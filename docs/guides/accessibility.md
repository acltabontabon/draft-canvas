# Accessibility

Use Draft Canvas with a keyboard, a screen reader, touch or a pen.

Shortcuts use Mac keys. On Windows and Linux, use `Ctrl` for `⌘` and `Alt` for `⌥`.

## Keyboard

`⌘K` runs any command, `?` lists shortcuts, and `Shift+F10` opens the context menu (the canvas menu
when nothing is selected). See [keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-and-commands.md).

The canvas is one `Tab` stop: `⌥` + arrow keys move the selection, and the **Outline** reaches every
connector. Finishing a shape's name, or closing a toolbar or panel from the keyboard, puts focus
back on the canvas. In **Appearance**, `←` and `→` switch tabs, and `Home` and `End` jump to the
first or last tab.

## Screen readers

**Outline** (`⌥O`) shows the view as a tree of shapes, boundaries and connectors, in sync with the
canvas. Names, kinds, relationships and selection changes are announced. The **Flows** panel is a
tree too: arrows move between flows, and `→` and `←` open and close one onto its steps.

## Touch and pen

Tap to select, press and hold for the context menu, and pinch to zoom. On touch screens, handles,
buttons, menu rows and status bar controls are at least 40 px. To draw with a pen or a finger, see
[Sketch and Smart Ink](sketching.md).

## Contrast and motion

Shape outlines and connectors reach at least 3:1 contrast in both themes, and interface text 4.5:1.

With the system's reduce-motion setting on, camera moves jump instead of animating, and Outage notes
and connector traces drop their movement.
