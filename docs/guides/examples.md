# Architecture examples

Starters are ready-made diagrams: Monolith, Microservices, Event-Driven, CQRS, sagas, data
pipelines and more. Each one is an ordinary diagram with flows. Add it and it's yours to change.

They live in a public repository,
[draft-canvas-starters](https://github.com/acltabontabon/draft-canvas-starters), so they improve
between releases. Each starter's page there shows its picture, when to use it and what to watch
out for.

## Load the starters gallery

Starters are off until you ask for them. On a blank canvas, choose **Browse the starters gallery**,
or press `⌘K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows and Linux) and run **Browse starters…**. Then choose **Load
starters**.

Loading reads the repository from GitHub. Nothing about your diagrams is sent. The gallery is then
kept on this device, so it works offline.

**Try an example** works without the gallery: the Order processing example ships with the app.

## Add a starter

- **On a blank canvas**, click one of the five suggested starters.
- **In the Starters dialog**, filter by kind or search, then pick a starter to preview it. Choose
  **Add to canvas**, or **Open as new diagram** to keep it apart from what you're drawing.
- **From `⌘K`**, type a starter's name and press `Enter`.

One `⌘Z` removes a starter you added.

## When a starter improves

The Starters dialog checks for changes when you open it, once a day, or when you choose the refresh
button. Starters that are new or have changed are marked **New** or **Updated** until you look at
them.

A diagram you made from a starter is never changed for you. If that starter has been updated, a
note at the top of the diagram offers **Open the new version**, which opens it as a new diagram
beside yours. **Dismiss** hides the note until the starter changes again.

## Stop loading starters

At the bottom of the Starters dialog, choose **Stop loading starters**. Draft Canvas forgets the
downloaded starters and makes no more requests. Diagrams you made from them stay as they are.

## Contribute a starter

See the repository's
[contributing guide](https://github.com/acltabontabon/draft-canvas-starters/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
A starter is a `.draftcanvas` file you draw in Draft Canvas, plus a short `starter.json`.
