An architecture canvas for developers

Make your
system clear.

Draw how your software works. Keep code and decisions beside the diagram, then walk your team through it.

Open web editor ↗ Explore examples ↓

Free and open source under Apache 2.0.

  • Technology-aware shapes

    Set a shape’s technology and get suggested connector labels, like “reads / writes” from an API to PostgreSQL.

  • Look inside a system

    Move from a C4 system context to its containers and components without leaving the diagram.

  • Keep decisions with the diagram

    Attach code and notes where they matter. Mark open questions where the team can find them.

  • Collaborate live

    Invite teammates to view or edit the same canvas. See their pointers, selections, and edits as they happen.

  • Present flows step by step

    Use Presentation Mode to walk through a flow, highlighting the relevant shapes and connectors at each step.

  • Bring your own agent

    Connect your coding agent to Draft Canvas Desktop via MCP. Review its proposed edits on the canvas.

  • Keep your files on your device

    Work in the browser or on Desktop. Save editable diagram files you own, without an account.
